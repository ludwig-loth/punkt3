## Why Competence Descriptions Often Fail - Starting Point and Motivation

![Test caption](/images/projects/kompetenzeditor/block-undefined.png)

Competence descriptions are fundamental for communicating learning objectives at universities and educational institutions. They provide essential guidance not only for students, who need clarity regarding the skills and knowledge expected of them, but also for educators, who must clearly define and assess learning outcomes. In practice, however, such descriptions are often created under less-than-ideal circumstances. Authors, typically educators without specialized training in creating precise learning outcomes, frequently struggle to draft clear, comprehensible texts. As a result, descriptions tend to be overly general, ambiguous, or even misleading, making it difficult for students to understand expectations and challenging educators in assessing the intended competencies.

The motivation behind developing this editor arose from this specific problem. The goal was to create a tool that directly assists educators in formulating high-quality competence descriptions that are precise, clear, and unequivocal.

## Innovation and Special Features

![](/images/projects/kompetenzeditor/block-undefined.png)

### An Innovative Approach: NLP-powered Editor Specifically for German Texts

The main innovation of the Competence Editor, which sets the application apart from others, is its use of Natural Language Processing (NLP) for the real-time analysis of German-language competence formulations. NLP is an area of artificial intelligence that deals with the machine processing of natural language. To date, most NLP tools have concentrated on the English language, meaning that there are hardly any comparable solutions for German-speaking countries.

This editor closes this gap by directly analyzing the quality of German texts. It automatically recognizes keywords and problematic formulations and offers users immediate, context-sensitive suggestions for improvement. The analysis is based on scientifically sound taxonomies and evaluation criteria, providing users with precise information on how they can optimize their texts. This not only improves linguistic quality, but also ensures that the skills described are clearly measurable and verifiable. Which is a key requirement in academic teaching.

![Example excerpt of the help](/images/projects/kompetenzeditor/block-undefined.png)

### Dynamic Assistance and Intuitive User Experience

The competence editor provides a comprehensive user experience, allowing easy and intuitive creation or refinement of competence descriptions. Dynamic, real-time analysis highlights problematic phrases and formulations directly within the text, offering clear, actionable advice for improvement alongside concrete alternatives. Additionally, the editor presents a percentage-based quality score, giving users instant insight into the overall quality of their text.

The clear, user-friendly interface has been specifically designed to facilitate easy access for users regardless of their technical background. Accompanied by detailed explanations and help texts, the editor ensures straightforward use and broad acceptance.

## My Role - Independent Conceptualization and Complete Implementation

![](/images/projects/kompetenzeditor/block-undefined.png)

As the sole developer of this project, my role was comprehensive and demanding: From independently developing the conceptual framework to fully implementing the technical aspects using modern web technologies and integrating complex NLP methods, I executed all tasks autonomously. Although the initial idea emerged from a professional discussion, the entire conceptual design, methodological refinement, and practical implementation were solely my own. My personal motivation was to create a practical, scientifically grounded solution for a frequently occurring yet inadequately addressed challenge in higher education. I was especially fascinated by the opportunity to directly transform theoretical knowledge into a useful tool benefiting both educators and students.
