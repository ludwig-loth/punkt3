## What is PUNKT3 ?

![](/images/projects/punkt3/block-5.png)

![portrait](/images/landing/portrait.jpg)

**PUNKT3 (Punkte) \[ˈpʊŋktə]** is a template for your personal website built with Nuxt and Tailwind CSS.

Punkte is the german word for "points" or "dots". The whole design philosophy of this frontend template is based on dots. The number 3 represents the three main aspects of the framework:

- Personal website
- Portfolio
- Blog

It presents a new design that captivates with a **gentle retro-brutalist style**. Every detail has been carefully thought through to ensure user-friendly operation. On desktop and mobile devices.

## Responsive Design for Optimal Usability

![Screenshot of the Projects menu item, in the mobile view](/images/projects/punkt3/block-5.png)

*PUNKT3 was optimized for all display sizes right from the start.*

Especially in web applications, it is important to ensure flexible adaptation to different screen sizes right from the start and to implement this accordingly.

That's why PUNKT3 has a maximum width so that users don't have to read lines that are too long. At the same time, it makes effective use of the available space by positioning the two menus generously next to the actual content. As soon as the available display area shrinks, the submenu automatically moves below the main menu to make enough room for the content.

For the smallest display sizes, such as smartphones, the menus are combined into a menu button. It is particularly noteworthy that this button is located in the lower right corner and not, as is unfortunately still common, in one of the upper corners. By positioning it at the bottom right, the menu button remains easily accessible even when operating with one hand.

## Soft retro brutalism for a pleasant user experience

![Screenshot of a sample project.](/images/projects/punkt3/block-5.png)

With my own concept of “soft retro brutalism,” I deliberately wanted to create a pleasant and friendly user experience. Classic brutalism often offers clear structures, but frequently comes across as aggressive and exhausting. My approach, on the other hand, combines a tidy and clear design with soft, rounded shapes, subtle animations, and pastel-colored accents. This creates a cozy atmosphere and encourages users to stay in the application longer.

I deliberately decided against a so-called one-page application, as this can often lead to “scroll fatigue.” Instead, I structured Podinu clearly and divided it into several pages to direct users' attention to relevant content. While there is a certain risk of “click fatigue,” I countered this with reduced use of click interactions and varied hover animations that specifically encourage interaction. Apart from page changes and the mobile menu, all information is visible at all times, so nothing remains hidden or has to be made visible through additional clicks.

I chose dots as a recurring design element because they are easy to implement and not overly common in web design. I also work specifically with three main colors to emphasize interactivity and orientation:

- **Red** always signals interaction possibilities and thus intuitively draws attention to clickable elements.
- **Orange** marks important but non-interactive elements, such as selected states or highlighted information.
- **Blue** is primarily used for decorative purposes and helps to visually distinguish individual components.

In addition, I guide users through the application with eye-catching dotted lines. These visual paths highlight interactive or related elements and ensure that content can be easily and quickly understood.

A dark mode could be added as an extra option in the future.
