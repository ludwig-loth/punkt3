The website for the Effelder Kirmes was specially developed and technically implemented for this funfair. It is based on a customized WordPress theme that was created as part of a volunteer project.

The complete support, from the conception to the hosting to the ongoing administration of the underlying WordPress instance, is provided on a voluntary basis. During the implementation, I was able to acquire and deepen my extensive knowledge of the structure of WordPress and the development of my own plugins.

In this short project, I was also able to express my creative side. All graphic and design elements, including all images and illustrations, were created manually by me. The conceptual direction and visual design are also entirely my own work.

Particular attention was paid to a clearly structured and easy-to-read presentation of the content. The design is consistent and user-friendly throughout, with the aim of presenting the character of the funfair in an appropriate digital format and offering visitors easy access to relevant information.

After several years of use, I am planning a new version of the website in the future. This future version should simplify central functions and make the content more contemporary and representative in order to meet the growing requirements and further develop the digital presence of the funfair in the long term.
